The two modes

HOME + PLAY switches between Bardic Crafting (write songs on four tracks) and Encounter.

In Encounter, white keys 1, 5, 9 and 13 are the scene groups: rest, explore, combat and climax. The sharps after each group key play its variations; hold the group key for the fourth variation. The other white keys bend the battle.

HOME + REC, held: purge a loop (Bardic) or fall silent (Encounter).

A MIDI keyboard?

Plug one in and press . Program change 1-16 cues a scene; notes play the selected track.
