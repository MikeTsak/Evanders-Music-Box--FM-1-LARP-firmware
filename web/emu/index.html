The two modes

HOME + PLAY switches between Bardic Crafting (compose, four tracks) and Encounter (white keys cue scenes on the next bar, black keys bend the battle).

HOME + REC, held: purge a loop (Bardic) or fall silent (Encounter).

The full field manual: MANUAL.md.

A MIDI keyboard?

Plug one in and press . Program change 1-16 cues a scene; notes play the selected track.
