Mother Melody gave the First Song no words, only a rhythm and a key. Whoever holds this Box holds a sliver of that Song. In a quiet hour it is a workshop where songs are made. When steel is drawn it is a herald that knows the mood of the field before the bard does. These pages teach both.

I. Knowing the Box

Every control on the FM-1 has a purposeful role in the bard's hands.

In Bardic Crafting they open pages. In Encounter they alter intensity, tempo, reverb hall, and music level.

27 Keyboard Keys

  • 11 Black Keys: Marked OP1 OP2 OP3 OP4 OP5 OP6 PIT GLO MONO POLY and unlabelled TOP. Ghost drum hits in Crafting; the 11 variations of the chosen scene in Encounter.

The Awakening (Boot Sequence)

At power-on the screen paints the white-and-blue curved monogram of Mother Melody with flowing musical waves. As the 6-note physical harp chime resounds, the Blue Rose blooms, proclaiming:

EVANDER'S MUSIC BOX — MOTHER MELODY HEARS THY SONG

A light wave sweeps across the keys. Tapping any button after 0.5 s skips the animation. The Box wakes exactly where you left it: the same mode, track and Scale Lock, with every song and loop (it keeps itself every 30 seconds).

Scale Lock: Playing Like a Harmonica

The Scale Lock holds every note of the Box in one scale: the keys, the sequencer, the Encounter scenes, freestyle and MIDI notes. With it on, a wrong note cannot be played. Hold GLO and the glass shows the keyboard with what each key sets; while GLO is held, keys do not play.

Hold GLO +Sets
a key from F3 to E4 (the left twelve, white and black)the root: the key's own note
F4 · G4 · A4 · B4MAJOR · MINOR · DORIAN · PHRYGIAN
C5 · D5 · E5LYDIAN · MIXOLYDIAN · HARMONIC MINOR
F5 · G5PENTATONIC MAJOR · PENTATONIC MINOR
F#4 · G#4 (black)DIMINISHED (whole-half) · PHRYGIAN DOMINANT
F#5 (black)lock off
ENV (button)lock off: back to the normal scale of the song or scene
  • Sequenced notes and scenes move into the lock's scale degree by degree, so a melody keeps its shape; a scene keeps its own register.
  • Press the same root key again to step through the scales: C MAJOR → C MINOR → C DORIAN → Phrygian → Lydian → Mixolydian → harmonic → penta major → penta minor → diminished → Phrygian dominant, then round again. A new root keeps the scale.
  • Live keys outside the scale play the scale note just below. Drums are untouched; timpani and gong tune to the root.
  • While the lock is on, GLO is half-lit and the header reads LOCK with the key. Tapping GLO alone still opens the Modes page.

The Knob HUD

Turn any of K1–K4, on any page, in either mode, and a card in the knob's own colour names exactly what it changes and shows the value (TRACK 2 LEVEL, REVERB DECAY, FILTER CUTOFF, INTENSITY…). It leaves a second after the knob stops.

Keeping Songs (Autosave)

You never need to save the workbench. Every 30 seconds, if anything changed, the Box keeps every step, loop, instrument and setting (Scale Lock and Modern Mode included), plus the mode, track, octaves, last scene, tempo and music volume. At power-on it wakes exactly there; the footer says KEPT: AUTOSAVED after each save.

Clearing a flash sector silences the FM-1 for a few hundredths of a second, so the Box clears sectors only while stopped and untouched, and saves into blank ones while you play. Only after two minutes of unsaved changes without ever stopping will it take that short gap mid-song.

GestureEffect
Hold SAVE 1 sKeep everything now
Hold SAVE + white keyBind the workbench to that Encounter scene (1–16)

IV. Encounter: the Herald

When the Box is strapped to the forearm in armor or robes, Evander does not compose. He calls. Each white key is a mood of the field; once one sounds, the sharps bend it. The Box waits for the bar line, then turns the music as a minstrel troupe would.

The Variations (Black Keys)

Once a scene is chosen (playing or queued), all eleven sharps become its variations; before that they stay dark and do nothing. A variation lands on the next beat, and one pressed while a scene is queued waits for that scene. Choosing a new scene clears them all. A variation's sharp stays lit while it holds.

A change of mood keeps each note's place in the scale: the same tune, a new colour. Under a Scale Lock the moods and key changes leave the pitch alone; the rest still work.

Stealth Mode for Night Ambushes

Configure on Box page (GLO → GLO → K3):

  • GLOW (Default): Full reactive scale lighting and ambient cues.
  • LIT: Removes ambient background glow; only active functional keys illuminate.
  • DARK (Stealth): Blackout for covert night raids. Only playing scene, queue blink, beat pulse, and active variations shine.

On a Computer (USB Audio + MIDI)

Plug the FM-1 into a computer with a data cable. It appears as a USB audio input and a USB MIDI port, class compliant: no driver on Windows, macOS or Linux. In a DAW or Audacity, choose it as the recording input and you record exactly what the Box plays (16-bit stereo, 44.1 or 48 kHz). MASTER and the OUTPUT ceiling apply to the recording too. The port is named Felucca, after the hardware layer the Box is built on.

VII. When the Box Falters

TroubleRemedy
Too quiet outdoors Turn MASTER past halfway: it engages up to +9.5 dB makeup gain through the peak limiter. Set OUTPUT to LOUD (GLO → GLO → K4).
Crackles at maximum volume Set OUTPUT to NORM (-3 dBFS) or SAFE (-6 dBFS) for sensitive external portable speakers.
Silence after a drop or bump Turn MASTER up. Press PLAY. Press OCT- + OCT+ to restore Full Host.
Corrupted loop in battle Hold HOME + REC for 0.4 s to purge. Tap HOME + SAVE to undo if needed.
Notes you did not expectA Scale Lock may be on (GLO half-lit): GLO + ENV frees the keys.
A sharp does nothing in EncounterChoose a scene first (a white key): the sharps are its variations.
An instrument you did not expectModern Mode may be on: HOME + FX + GLO turns it off.
The computer does not see the audioUse a data cable; in the DAW pick the FM-1 (Felucca) as the input; try 44.1 kHz.
Pink diagnostic line at boot Appears only when USB-connected to diagnose past crashes: LOW VOLTAGE (recharge battery), WATCHDOG or CRASHED (report with current activity), or STOPPED AT STEP n. (Hidden on battery power).
Return to stock firmware Open The Installer and use Restore V15 with official M-VAVE stock firmware.
Deep recovery mode Hold OCT- + OCT+ for 5 seconds on power-on to invoke the JieLi hardware bootloader (UBOOT).

A bard's last trick: when every string is cut, hum. Mother Melody hears that too.
