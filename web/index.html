Live Action Roleplay

Built Specifically to Play a Bard in a LARP

Most synthesizers belong on a studio desk or a stage stand. Evander's Box was forged for the mud, costumes, leather armor, and campfires of live roleplay.

Forearm-Mounted Scoring

Strapped to your forearm, the tactile 27-key panel can be played in leather bracers or gloves. Change the battle soundtrack seamlessly on the next bar without taking your eyes off the skirmish.

Authentic Medieval Vibes

20 historical acoustic engines: Hurdy-Gurdy with buzzing wheel, Phys Harp, Lute, Bagpipes, Shawm, Psaltery, Cathedral Choir, and Bodhrán war drums. No modern electronic beeps… unless a bard knows the secret chord.

Pure Acoustic Sound

There are no recorded wind, fire, or bird sound effects. You are already standing in the real forest and around the real campfire—every ounce of DSP power goes into resonant physical instruments.

Loud Enough for Outdoors

Featuring +9.5 dB master makeup gain and an intelligent look-ahead peak limiter. Your bardic songs ring out clearly across outdoor clearings and battlefields without distortion.

16 Live Battle Scenes

Each of the 16 white keys is a soundtrack scene, from tavern rest to boss fight; once one plays, the 11 black keys bend it. Tap SEQ for Freestyle and every key plays a melody over the scene, never a wrong note.

Night Ambush Stealth

Reactive LEDs light only notes in the current modal scale. For covert night raids, activate Stealth Mode to black out ambient glow while keeping active triggers visible.

The Two Ways of Evander's Box

A single chord, HOME + PLAY, flips the FM-1 between two dedicated operational modes without dropping a single beat:

  • Bardic Crafting: A 4-track medieval sequencer workshop (Lead, Drone, Strings, War Drums). 64 steps, parameter locks, microtiming, Pythagorean/meantone temperaments, live looping, and the safety loop purge (HOME+REC).
  • Encounter Herald: The live LARP performance mode. 16 white keys = 16 scenes, 11 black keys = variations of the chosen scene (Phrygian dread, key changes, double/half time, breakdowns, stingers), Freestyle over the music (SEQ), cathedral swell (FX) and instant dialogue ducking (ARP).
  • In both: Scale Lock (hold GLO + a key) keeps every note in one scale like a harmonica; autosave every 30 s wakes the Box where you left it; USB audio + MIDI records straight into a DAW, no driver.
