Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (WebMIDI with SysEx). Firefox and Safari cannot do this.
  • A data USB-C cable, plugged straight into the computer. Close every other music app (DAWs, M-UPGRADE, other tabs that use MIDI).
  • Charge the FM-1. Do not unplug it while it writes; if it is interrupted it waits in update mode and this page resumes.
  • Coming from another custom firmware? The community advice is to return to official V15 first (below), then install.
  • Already running Evander's Box? Save your scenes with the Preset Console (Backup all) first.
  • This is hobby firmware, installed at your own risk. Recovery steps are in the README.
